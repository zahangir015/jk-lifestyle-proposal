Cloud posture

Hybrid-leaning-cloud

Managed cloud for applications and data, with local resilience where clinical / outlet continuity requires it.

Security posture

Health + finance are privileged domains

RBAC, 2FA for staff, encryption, consent records, auditability, VAPT before major go-lives.

Event posture

Capture useful events from day one

Operational events feed alerts, analytics and the future Data / AI layer without retrofitting history later.

03

Technology

10

Risk controls

Make known failure modes visible before they become schedule slips.

Hiring, dirty legacy data, workflow churn, scope, vendor APIs and security are treated as program risks, not engineering surprises.

11

Three-year operating view

The cost profile changes as the platform matures.

Development dominates early; infrastructure and operating maturity become the major recurring concerns as adoption grows.

Year 1

Build the operating backbone

Foundation, ERP/POS, Clinical and Website. Highest one-time development intensity; lower infrastructure usage.

Year 2

Scale digital products

Super App, Community, Franchise, Data/AI. Development continues while cloud, media and AI consumption rise.

Year 3

Operate, optimize, evolve

Steady-state enhancement and support. Infrastructure becomes the largest recurring line as usage compounds.
